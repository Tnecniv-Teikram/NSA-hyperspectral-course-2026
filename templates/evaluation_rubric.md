# Mini project evaluation rubric

**Space Academy 2026 · Hyperspectral course · Thursday 26 November — final submission and presentation**

The mini project is **100 % of the course grade**.

The rubric uses the five criteria of the 813 Challenge (Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation),
refined for the course mini project. Each criterion is scored 1–4. The weights sum to 100.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Excellent |
|-----------|:------:|---------------|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| **🔬 Validity** — scientific and technical rigour | **30** | No validation, or train/test overlap; preprocessing errors (bad bands or clouds left in) | Some metric reported, but a random pixel split or no baseline; limitations not discussed | Spatially independent validation, appropriate metrics + confusion matrix/residuals, baseline comparison, main limitations discussed | Everything in 3, plus uncertainty quantified (CIs, σ maps), sensitivity analysis, failure cases explained physically |
| **🎯 Relevance** — fit to theme, completeness, feasibility | **20** | Weak link between problem and output; notebook does not run | Output relates to the problem but is incomplete; runs with manual fixes | Complete, reproducible workflow that answers the stated problem | Also demonstrates a realistic path to operational use (scaling, repeat acquisitions, cost) |
| **🌍 Impact** — who benefits and how much | **20** | User and decision not identified | User named, benefit vague | Clear user, decision and benefit; output format suits the user | Benefit quantified (area, cost, time saved, people affected); policy or business relevance argued |
| **💡 Creativity** — originality and use of hyperspectral information | **15** | Only broadband indices a multispectral sensor could compute, without discussion | Uses hyperspectral bands, but the advantage is not demonstrated | Uses specific spectral features (absorption features, narrow-band indices, unmixing, detection) and explains them | Novel combination of methods/data, or a convincing demonstration of what only hyperspectral data can do |
| **🎤 Presentation** — clarity of storytelling and visuals | **15** | Hard to follow; unreadable figures | Understandable, but overloaded or unfocused | Clear story (problem → data → method → results → so what), good figures, on time | Compelling and memorable; excellent visuals; confident answers to questions |

**Score** = Σ (score / 4 × weight). Example: Validity 3, Relevance 4, Impact 3, Creativity 2, Presentation 3
→ 22.5 + 20 + 15 + 7.5 + 11.25 = **76.25 / 100**.
